# LiliuxFlow brand

Name: **LiliuxFlow**. Attribution: **LiliuxFlow by Diurnoctra**.
English positioning: **Local LLM serving and operations for Apple Silicon**.

The original LiliuxFlow mark, wordmarks and favicon artwork are licensed under [Apache License 2.0](../../LICENSE), SPDX `Apache-2.0`. These presentation guidelines do not add conditions to that license. The license's trademark provision applies; upstream marks and third-party assets retain their original terms. Artwork provenance and original file identities are recorded in [asset provenance](asset-provenance.json).

The selected mark uses a pointed central petal, two spreading upper petals and two recurved lower lips around an open flower throat, with a short stem. These paths were drawn for this project. The light and dark variants are designed for 16/24/32/64 px display.

The mark may be used at 16 px; a wordmark needs a minimum 160 px width. Leave clear space at least one quarter of the mark's width. Use #293a33 on light surfaces and #ecf4ed on dark surfaces. Use a single solid color; do not add shadows, gradients, outlines, rotate, crop, or stretch. The transparent mark must have an accessible product name in the embedding component. The wordmark uses the platform system font without redistributing font files.

Place attribution in a readable footer or About panel, separate from the mark: administration uses LiteLLM; model management uses llama-swap; inference uses Lily. Keep upstream versions and license links. This is an independent product and does not imply upstream endorsement. The brand asset is distinct from account avatars. Personal images are excluded from these files and release screenshots.

Favicon and raster derivatives follow the selected vector. The welcome page and dashboard recipes use these assets.
